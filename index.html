Community Isolation Risk along the NH-7 Himalayan Transport Corridor

Prince (Lead Spatial Researcher) • Department of Geography, University of Delhi • Sept 2026

Zenodo Record PDF Paper

Evaluated accessibility exposure across 3,419 settlements within a 15 km NH-7 buffer. Multi-criteria framework: Road Accessibility (46.91%), Healthcare Proximity (20.10%), NH-7 Proximity (20.10%), Slope (8.62%), Elevation (4.27%). Validated with in-situ hazard ground-truthing in Rudraprayag (Mastura landslide corridor).
