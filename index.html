Research & Publications

Ongoing Thesis Sentinel-1 SAR Sentinel-2 Optical GLOF Hydrodynamics

AI-Based Monitoring and Future Prediction of High-Altitude Glacial Lakes for GLOF Risk Assessment in Himachal Pradesh

Reconstructed 34-year decadal lake expansion (1990–2024) across benchmark proglacial lakes (Samudra Tapu: 161.9 ha; Gepang Gath: 104.5 ha) fusing multi-temporal Landsat 5/7/8 and Sentinel-2 (10m) water indices. Modeled 3D bathymetric water storage (30.7M–55.5M m³) and Froehlich dam-breach peak outflow (Qp = 8,093–12,108 m³/s) to simulate downstream arrival times at Sissu Village and Atal Tunnel corridor (NH-3).

Status: Active Research (Undergraduate Dissertation, DU)

Community Isolation Risk along the NH-7 Himalayan Transport Corridor

Formulated an independent spatial gravity model evaluating village accessibility exposure across 3,419 rural settlements along the NH-7 corridor in Uttarakhand within a 15 km buffer. Integrated Copernicus GLO-30 DEM, OpenStreetMap networks, and in-situ hazard ground-truthing in Rudraprayag (Mastura landslide corridor).
